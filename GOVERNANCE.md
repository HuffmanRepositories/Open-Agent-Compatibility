# Governance

This project is in an early research stage. Governance should remain lightweight and transparent.

## Decision process

- Routine documentation and test fixes may be merged by maintainers after review.
- Scope-changing proposals should be discussed in an issue before implementation.
- New protocol or runtime proposals require explicit overlap analysis and are presumed out of scope unless they address a demonstrated gap.
- Material decisions should be recorded in docs/decisions/ as short decision records.

## Maintainer responsibilities

Maintainers should:
- preserve project boundaries and upstream ownership;
- require source/version evidence for mapping claims;
- review stale mappings and test failures;
- label maturity accurately;
- document conflicts of interest or affiliation where relevant.

## Maturity

This repository is research-stage. No formal certification or standards authority is claimed. The project may archive or deprecate work that is unmaintained, superseded upstream, or no longer useful.
